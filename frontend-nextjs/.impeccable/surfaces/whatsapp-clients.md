# Clientes de WhatsApp IA

Mode: Operate. Precise extension of the established private CRM, code-led; no new identity or concept round.

THESIS: staff administers client businesses and their agents without public self-registration. Directory of businesses leads to a single record, with four user-approved tabs: Agente, WhatsApp, Conversaciones, Consumo.
OWN-WORLD: inherit DESIGN.md and admin.css: pale canvas, white work surfaces, charcoal type, indigo actions, Poppins and compact controls.
STORY: create business, save approved knowledge, let its owner authorize Meta through an expiring link, test privately, activate deliberately. Connections, conversations and quota are scoped per business. Oktae's own bot remains separate.
FIRST VIEWPORT: back to CRM, heading and primary Dar de alta; searchable row directory or honest empty state. Record opens with name, state and four tabs, then labeled form groups. No metric cards or invented clients.
FORM: inherited directory/detail workspace; explicitly specified narrow extension, no seed. Unsaved changes prompt on leaving, loading/error states and disabled dependent actions. Tabs underline active selection; no decorative motion.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, and DESIGN.md.

Review desktop and mobile: usable fields, contained wide directory, responsive detail, visible feedback. QA examples are temporary and removed. The public owner page reveals only name/registered phone to a high entropy, hashed, revocable 30-minute bearer invitation. No access to CRM, no signup, no payment or messages during preview. Real customer Meta onboarding remains subject to Meta permissions and account eligibility.
